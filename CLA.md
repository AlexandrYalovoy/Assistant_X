```

```

The agreement then covers all your later contributions too. A company that contributes the work of its employees
writes to the author first: no e-mail address is published, so open an issue on GitHub
(https://github.com/AlexandrYalovoy/Assistant_X/issues) titled `private contact request`, and the author replies there
with a private channel (the same for users of the GitFlic mirror).
